The Festival

Every winter, one campus becomes the room where the future gets argued about — in labs, on stages, and across exhibition halls.

— Office of Techfest, IIT Bombay

Techfest began as a modest gathering of curious minds on the IIT Bombay campus and has grown into Asia's largest science and technology festival — a three‑day confluence of exhibitions, competitions, lectures, and workshops that draws students, researchers, and innovators from across the world.

The name for this edition, An Aetherial Renaissance, is a deliberate pairing: aetherial for the frontier sciences — AI, space, robotics, the not‑yet‑named — and renaissance for the festival's own history of rebirth, where each edition rebuilds the previous one's ambition from scratch.
